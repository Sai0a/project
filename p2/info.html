This website provides information, resources, and updates related to disaster management in the North Eastern region of India. It aims to raise awareness, share safety guidelines, and connect people with official agencies and emergency support systems.
The website is developed and maintained by the SAGA as part of an initiative to promote disaster preparedness and response in the North East.
  • Details about different types of disasters common in the region
  • Preparedness and safety tips
  • Government policies and disaster management plans
  • Contact information for emergency services and relief agencies
  • Real-time alerts and weather updates
