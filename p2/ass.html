Quick history of Assam

Location: Northeastern India — South Of Eastern Himalayas

Assam — literally "land of the red river and blue hills" — is also known as the " gateway to the North East India" , famous for its biodiversity , including a large population of one- horned rhinos, and rich culture.situated in the foothills of the himalayas and is divided by the Brahmaputra Valley.Economically , Assam is globally recognized for its tea and was the first place for oil drilling in Asia

Detailed timeline & preparedness tips

  1. May–June 2025 monsoon crisis — intense rains triggered flash floods across many districts, with landslides reported and others. Relief operations and air-drops were used to help cut-off villages.
  2. Common impacts — damaged roads, washed-away bridges, destroyed crops, temporary sheltering in relief camps, and travel disruption along NH routes.
  3. Preparedness tips — keep an emergency bag (food, water, torch, first-aid), register with local disaster authorities, avoid river banks during heavy rain, and follow official evacuation orders.
  4. How you can help — support verified local relief funds/NGOs, donate through government-endorsed channels, or volunteer through district disaster management teams.
