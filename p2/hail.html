HAILSTORM

SERVICES WE PROVIDE

🛡

Phase 1: Immediate Response

RELIEF AND SAFETY

  • 📢 Weather Alerts & Warnings: Issuing timely and localized warnings (radio, mobile apps, sirens).
  • 🏠 Shelter Materials: Distributing tarpaulins and CGI sheets to cover damaged roofs and windows.
  • 🍎 Essential Supplies: Providing dry rations, clean water, blankets, and first-aid kits.
💰

Phase 2: Financial Recovery

DAMAGE AND FUNDING

  • 📈 Damage Assessment: Mobilizing teams for rapid assessment of damage to homes, infrastructure, and crops.
  • 💵 Disaster Funding: Managing and disbursing State/National Disaster Response Funds to affected regions.
  • 🔨 Individual Assistance: Providing direct financial aid for repair costs, crop loss, or replacement of damaged livestock.
📝

Phase 3: Mitigation & Plans

PREVENTION AND RESILIENCE

  • 📘 Safety Guidelines: Public information on securing outdoor items, sheltering vehicles, and making emergency kits.
  • 🗺 Developing Action Plans: Creating comprehensive plans for prevention and management of severe weather events.
  • 🏗 Reconstruction Support: Offering aid for rebuilding homes and infrastructure to be more resilient against future hazards.

The disaster agency manages the full lifecycle of a hailstorm event, from *pre-warning to full recovery*.
